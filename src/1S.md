# Transfer 1♠

Responses follow [Moscito 2007][M2007] §2.1.4.  Point ranges are copied from
its 9--14 openings.  Pass-or-correct bids cater for opener's possible clubs.

| 1♠ - | |
|------|-|
| 1NT! | 11+
| 2♣!  | 5+♠, 4+♥
| 2♦!  | TRF, 5+♥
| 2♥!  | TRF, 5+♠
| 2♠!  | TRF, 5+♣
| 2NT! | LIM, ♦ support
| 3♣!  | P/C
| 3♦   | 6--9, 3--4♦
| 3♥♠! | SPL
| 4♣!  | SPL

Over 1NT opener bids 2♣♦♥♠ naturally, and responder's next step is a relay.
Over 2♣ responder's 2NT is a good 3-card heart raise.  Over 2♠ opener's 2NT
shows short clubs.  Over 2NT opener's new suit shows shortage.

| 1♠ - 2♦! - | |
|------|-|
| 2♥   | 0--2♥
| 2NT! | good raise, 3+♥
| 3♥   | WK raise, 3+♥

| 1♠ - 2♦! -<br>2♥ - | |
|------|-|
| 2♠   | 5+♥, 4=♠
| 2NT! | G/T
| 3♣!  | P/C

| 1♠ - 2♦! -<br>2♥ - 2NT! - | |
|-----|-|
| 3♣♦ | MIN, #
| 3♥♠ | MAX, ♣/♦

Over 2♥ opener rebids the same way with spades as the suit.
