# Minor Multi 1♠

I find it too difficult to have all the following:

- A strong 1NT relay
- Forcing major suits
- Preemptive major suits

This is a major-oriented system, so I give up the strength of the 1NT relay.
When responder lacks 5-card major, they are usually interested in minor suits
regardless of strength.

Note that the spade transfer is 2♣ because it needs more space to handle both
5-5 and 5-4 hands.

Lebensohl 2NT is home to 3-level one-suited preempts.  It paradoxically asks the
worse minor suit to pass or correct.
