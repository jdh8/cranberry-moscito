# Strong 1♣

> \[O]ther strong club systems respond 1♥ up with game going hands and 1♦ with
> the rest.  This approach is clearly wrong.  About 19% of responding hands are
> junk, 45% are semi positives and the remaining 36% are positives.
>
> ---Paul Marston, [MOSCITO 2005](https://bridgewithdan.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/moscito2005intro.pdf)
