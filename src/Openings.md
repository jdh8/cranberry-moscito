# Openings

One-of-a-suit and 1NT openings split the HCP scale into three zones, from
strongest to weakest:

- **Strong (16+):** Open 1♣.
- **Heavy (13--15):** Always open: balanced hands in 1NT, unbalanced hands in
  a suit.
- **Light (11--12):** Open all eligible 12-counts.  Select 11-counts with
  5-card major or good playing strength.

[Precision 2♣][2♣] takes 6+♣ in the limited zones since 1♠ promises diamonds.

The 2♦♥♠ openings handle the **preemptive 4--10** zone separately.  They are the
same as in *Strawberry Polish Club*: [Multi 2♦][2♦], [Muiderberg 2♥♠][2M], and
[unusual 2NT][2NT].

## Choice between suits

Choice between major suits is similar to Transfer Walsh responses.

1. Transfer to the longer major suit
2. Transfer to spades with 5+♠ and 5+♥
3. Transfer to hearts with 4=♠ and 4=♥

Pick a major suit over a minor suit most of the time, but open 1♠ for the minor
suit when you have 6+ cards in a minor suit and only 4 cards in a major suit.

|      | 4=♠♥   | 5+♠♥ |
|------|--------|------|
| 5=♦♣ | 1♦♥    | 1♦♥  |
| 6+♦♣ | 1♠, 2♣ | 1♦♥  |

## The notrump ladder

- **11--12:** 1♦♥♠
- **13--15:** 1NT
- **16--18:** 1♣ - 1♦ - 1NT
- **19--21:** 1♣ - 1♦ - 1♥
- **22--24:** 1♣ - 1♦ - 2NT
- **Game-forcing:** 1♣ - 1♦ - 1♥
