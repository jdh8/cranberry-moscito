# Transfer 1♦

Responses follow [Moscito 2007][M2007] §2.1.2 and §3.3.  The 1♥ relay carries
three hand types: a natural 1NT response, a game try in hearts or notrump,
and any 13+.

| 1♦ - | |
|------|-|
| 1♥!  | (R), CONST+
| 1♠   | F, 4+♠
| 1NT! | NF TRF, 5+♣
| 2♣!  | NF TRF, 5+♦
| 2♦!  | CONST+, 3+♥
| 2♥!  | PRE, 3+♥
| 2♠   | PRE, 6+♠
| 2NT! | LIM+, 4+♥
| 3♣♦  | PRE, 6+#
| 3♥   | PRE, 4+♥
| 3♠!  | SPL, 0--1#, 4+♥
| 3NT! | BAL COG, 4+♥
| 4♣♦! | SPL, 0--1#, 4+♥

Over 2NT opener's new suit is a slam try.  Over 3NT opener's new suit shows
shortage.
